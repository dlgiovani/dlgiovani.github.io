## Zeno Rocha
[zenorocha.com](https://zenorocha.com)\
\
Zeno is a Brazilian software engineer, currently founder and CEO of Resend. His _portfolio_ is a work of art that grabs your attention, developers' especially.

![Zeno Rocha's Portfolio](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1100/format:webp/1*J3dpMPTQHdT_6aSuGbmYgA.png)

## Heather Vandervecht
[heathervv.com](https://heathervv.com)
